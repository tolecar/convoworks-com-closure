2019–2026

Convoworks is closed.

A visual workflow engine for WordPress — voice, APIs, webhooks, later GPT.
Built by ZEF Development d.o.o. in Varaždin.

You designed a service once and ran it inside the CMS you already had. The first version was a voice-app builder, co-financed by the European Union and backed by Fil Rouge Capital. The smart-speaker market did not hold. The later work on AI inside WordPress was real; it did not become a business that could carry the company.

The product is discontinued. There will be no further releases or support. Existing installs keep running on your own servers — they are yours. The WordPress.org listing was closed in 2025. Public source remains on GitHub.

If you followed an old docs, blog, or download link, this is now the whole site.

Timeline

  1. 2019

    Work begins. EU co-financed the first phase.

  2. 2021

    Convoworks WP ships. Voice skills, then a broader workflow runtime.

  3. 2023

    Voice market gone. The same engine is pointed at AI in WordPress.

  4. 2025

    The plugin directory listing is closed. Source stays on GitHub.

  5. 2026

    Project discontinued. The company is winding down.
