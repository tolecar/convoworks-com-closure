Convoworks

2019–2026

Closed.

Convoworks was a visual workflow engine for WordPress. You designed a service once — voice, APIs, webhooks, later GPT — and ran it inside the CMS you already had.

It was built by ZEF Development d.o.o. in Varaždin, Croatia, with support from the European Union and Fil Rouge Capital. The smart-speaker market did not hold. The later work on AI inside WordPress was real; it did not become a business that could carry the company.

The product is discontinued. There will be no further releases or support. Existing installs keep running on your own servers — they are yours. The WordPress.org listing was closed in 2025. Public source remains on GitHub.

If you followed an old documentation, blog, or download link, this is now the whole site.

  1. 2019

    Work begins. EU co-financed the first phase.

  2. 2021

    Convoworks WP ships. Voice skills, then a broader workflow runtime.

  3. 2023

    Voice market gone. The same engine is pointed at AI in WordPress.

  4. 2025

    The plugin directory listing is closed. Source stays on GitHub.

  5. 2026

    Project discontinued. The company is winding down.
